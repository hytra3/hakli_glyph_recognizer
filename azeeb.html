Azeeb

The company behind the Hakli Glyph Recognizer and its sister tools for documenting the languages and written heritage of Dhofar.

Azeeb builds software in service of endangered-language communities — recognizing and archiving ancient Dhofari inscriptions, and keeping the resulting record in the hands of the community it belongs to.

The Hakli Glyph Recognizer is one of those tools.
