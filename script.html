Dhofari Hakli 𐪀𐪁𐪂𐪃

Dhofari Script 1a, v0.1. Letter forms are traced from the KMG 120-126 and KMD 28-31 abecedaries in Al-Jallad (2025), JEOL 49, Chart 1. Letters are encoded on the Old North Arabian block by sound value.

Notes

  • Merged letters. The paper finds no separate signs for ẓ/ṣ, ṯ/s³ and ḏ/z. Each merged pair shares one glyph, and both codepoints of the pair show it.
  • Direction. Old North Arabian is a right-to-left block, so text runs right to left by default. Glyph shapes are as published. Nothing is mirrored.
  • Source resolution. The sign images in the paper are about 50 px, so the outlines keep some of the original brush texture. Cleaner tracings from photographs would sharpen them.
  • Files. OTF for desktop installs · WOFF2 for the web · mapping.json. The font is rebuilt with tools/build_dhofari_font.py.
  • Not yet included: Script 1b and Script 2, the word divider, and the unidentified sign ("?") in the HGR glyph set.
